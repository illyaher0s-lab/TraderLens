### Key Design Decisions
- HardFilterSnapshot fields use `bool | None` and `float | None` — `None` means "unknown, therefore not a pass"
- validate_candidate treats every None as a blocking unknown flag
- Evidence API extracts hard_filter_metadata from HardFilterSnapshot and passes it through to EvidenceLightRunner
- EvidenceLightRunner records metadata in tool_trace[0] and copies gaps into evidence_gaps
- Data-source gaps are NEVER silently passed — every None produces an unknown_* flag
- verification_id is read from candidate.verification_id (not args); missing/expired/mismatched are rejected
